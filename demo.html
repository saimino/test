      <h2>Abstract</h2>

      <p>

        MidiLM is a 1.67B-parameter language model for text-to-MIDI generation.

        It introduces a dual-path architecture with a Mixture of Experts decoder,

        enabling controllable and expressive symbolic music generation from natural language prompts.

      </p>
